About Ihsan Care

Ihsan Care is a Somali digital health platform delivering maternal and child care solutions. We integrate biometric verification, cashless care, and donor transparency systems to empower health programs and NGOs.

Our mission: Transform healthcare delivery with transparency, trust, and technology.

Mission

Improve maternal & child health through digital transparency and biometric verification.

Vision

Every mother and child empowered by secure, accountable digital health intelligence.

National Digital Health Infrastructure

Rebuilding Somalia’s
Maternal & Child Health System

Governance, Compliance & Accountability

Ihsan Care is committed to ethical operations, financial transparency, and internationally recognized standards for data protection, donor reporting, and program accountability.

Ihsan Families – Digital Health in Somalia

Ihsan Families, also known as Ihsan Care, is Somalia’s first digital health platform focused on maternal and child healthcare. The platform provides biometric medical identity, AI-powered risk prediction, donor transparency systems, and secure healthcare delivery across Jubaland and Somalia.
