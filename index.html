About Ihsan Care

Ihsan Care is a Somali digital health platform delivering maternal and child care solutions. We integrate biometric verification, cashless care, and donor transparency systems to empower health programs and NGOs.

Our mission: Transform healthcare delivery with transparency, trust, and technology.

Mission

Improve maternal & child health through digital transparency and biometric verification.

Vision

Every mother and child empowered by secure, accountable digital health intelligence.

Platform Capabilities

🔐 Biometric Identity

Fingerprint-based verification, secure QR validation, and national health ID issuance.

📊 Donor Transparency

Live dashboards, financial flow tracking, fraud alerts, and real-time impact reporting.

🧾 Cashless Care

Policy validation, digital claims, automated reimbursements, and clinic integration.

📱 Mobile Enrollment

Rapid enrollment via mobile devices, including maternal & child health data capture in remote areas.

🖥 AI Risk Scoring

Predictive analytics for maternal and child health risks, helping clinics prioritize high-risk cases.

🌍 Multi-Clinic Network

Connect multiple clinics in real-time, ensuring data integrity, centralized dashboards, and collaborative care.
